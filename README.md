# Neurons Through the Looking Glass

Constructing an Interacting Quantum Field in Two Dimensions

Cody Mitchell, Fractal Research Group · Version 2 · 6 November 2026

[Paper](paper/neurons-through-the-looking-glass-v2.pdf) · [Changes](CHANGES.md) · [Build instructions](REPRODUCE.md)

Two process-neuron architectures converge to the established massive,
weakly coupled two-dimensional Wick-quartic field under the stated classical
inputs. Their normalized finite-stage laws preserve reflection positivity
across the two central coordinate lines. One deterministic sequence works for
both architectures and compactly supported measurements chosen afterwards.

Version 2 brings the later conditional quantitative bounds and prescribed
renormalized local-square results into the manuscript. The quantitative results
select sufficient finite parameters for their stated observations and premises.
The local-square results bound target smoothing error and combine eight errors
in the joint field/local-square law. The exact domains, normalization and source
conditions appear in the paper.

Finite width counts whole random processes, with countably many internal random
variables globally. The original common sequence remains existential. The later
finite-observation choices do not establish a practical sampler, a useful total
work bound, computational advantage, broader models or historical priority.
The computational feasibility attempt remains incomplete and stopped.

This package contains the mathematical manuscript, its build source, reading
maps and version notes. Historical private review packets and symbolic software
implementations are not part of this export. Their named assumptions and source
access limits remain explicit; see [the companion index](paper/COMPANION-INDEX.md).
The source archive reproduces the document, not a field simulator.

The manuscript retains dated revision guides as a record of which result and
software versions each statement concerns. Their candidate and then-pending
status language is historical. [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) explains the accepted
baseline and the separate checks for this public edition.
